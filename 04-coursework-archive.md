## Google Cybersecurity Certificate — security audit project (Botium Toys)

A multi-part audit of a fictional toy company, working from audit scope through to a stakeholder-ready summary. Read in full and written up as portfolio page `05-botium-toys-security-audit.md`:

- **Botium Toys: Audit scope and goals** — scope covering the entire security program (user permissions, controls, procedures across accounting, endpoint detection, firewalls, IDS, SIEM); goals anchored on the NIST CSF and least privilege.
- **Botium Toys: Risk assessment** — asset inventory; inadequate asset management and missing controls; overall risk score 8/10 (high).
- **Controls assessment** — administrative, technical, and physical controls each assessed and prioritised (least privilege, disaster recovery, IDS, encryption, backups among the HIGH-priority gaps; firewall already in place).
- **Compliance checklist** — GDPR (72-hour breach notification) and PCI DSS worked through as the regimes applying to the company; FERC-NERC, HIPAA, and SOC 1/2 also reviewed.
- **Stakeholder memorandum** — signed memorandum from Isaac Osei Mensah, 24 August 2023, splitting findings into critical (address immediately) and routine, with the recommendation to act quickly.
